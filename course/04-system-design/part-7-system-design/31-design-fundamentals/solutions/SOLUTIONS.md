# Solutions & Commentary — Module 31: Design Fundamentals

## Key Takeaways
- Frame requirements into Functional (API contracts) vs Non-Functional (QPS, SLA/SLO, latency, availability).
- Back-of-the-envelope estimation: convert monthly operations to queries/sec and calculate disk/network bandwidth.
- Memorize latency numbers: L1 cache (~1ns), RAM (~100ns), SSD (~100µs), HDD (~10ms), Cross-region network (~150ms).
