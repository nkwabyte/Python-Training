# Solutions & Commentary — Module M11: The Transformer

## Key Takeaways
- Multi-Head Attention projects Queries, Keys, and Values into $H$ parallel subspaces.
- Causal masking (lower triangular boolean mask) prevents autoregressive language models from attending to future tokens.
- Positional encodings (Sinusoidal or RoPE) inject sequence order into permutation-invariant self-attention.
